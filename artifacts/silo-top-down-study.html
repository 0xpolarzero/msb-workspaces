The new visual grammar

This direction takes the spatial premise, not the television series’ promotional identity. No borrowed typography, key art, or title treatment.

1

Concentric levels

Each ring is a contained workspace level around one shared control core.

2

Central void

The open center creates depth and distinguishes the mark from a generic disc.

3

Rotating breaks

Staggered openings imply stairs, circulation, and descent instead of a target.

Four plan-view marks

All four abandon the agricultural silhouette. The difference is how literally they describe depth and circulation.

Recommended

Descent

Three broken levels rotate toward an open core. Architectural, compact, and distinctive.

Layered / dynamic / ownable

Shaft

A direct floor plan: rim, levels, and central well. Strong, but close to a target.

Calm / literal / static

Spiral

A continuous path descending inward. Expressive at scale, too intricate in the menu bar.

Narrative / organic / complex

Levels

Concentric floors connected by offset bridges. Technical, but resembles a mechanism.

Structural / precise / mechanical

Recommended direction

Descent carries the underground-silo idea without becoming fan art. The broken rings also map directly to several isolated workspaces around one app.

Preferred mark

Descent

The eye follows each opening clockwise and inward. That motion creates implied depth while the complete silhouette remains a simple circle.

  • Outer ring: containment and app boundary
  • Middle rings: independent workspace levels
  • Open core: depth, access, and isolation
  • Staggered gaps: circulation rather than a target

Status-item proof

Silo

Decision: this is the stronger identity territory. It is more elegant, more relevant to software workspaces, and no longer depends on recognizing a farm structure.

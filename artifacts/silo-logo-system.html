MicroSandbox identity direction

SILO / 01

Silo

A quiet control surface for isolated workspaces. One permanent mark; state appears around it.

Primary mark / adaptive monochrome

Logo construction

A literal silhouette reduced until it works as a status item. The dome and doorway identify a silo; the horizontal seams suggest stacked workspaces.

1Domed roof

The only agricultural cue. Shallow enough to remain architectural rather than illustrative.

2Stacked seams

Three contained levels imply several workspaces under one control surface.

3Open doorway

Prevents the mark from reading as a database, battery, or waste bin.

4Stable outline

The silhouette never changes. Status is always additive: badge, slash, or tint.

Complete state set

Normal is intentionally colorless. Color means the app is active or needs attention; badge geometry preserves meaning without color.

Healthy

Monochrome. No checkmark, badge, or green success noise.

Activity

Blue ring-dot while an operation is actively running.

Notification

A neutral dot records new information without implying failure.

Attention

Orange exclamation. Work can continue, but action is required.

Critical

Red cross badge for a blocking or failed condition.

Offline

Muted slash when current workspace state cannot be reached.

Repair required

Shares the orange warning grammar; tooltip names the repair.

Unavailable

Reduced contrast only when Silo itself cannot provide actions.

At menu-bar scale

The mark is designed at 20 px, then placed in an 18–22 pt status-item image. The healthy state adapts to the menu bar automatically.

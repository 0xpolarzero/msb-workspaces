Make it
a silo.

The last version solved height by sacrificing identity. The correction is a cylindrical body with a separate roof, not a flatter arch.

What went wrong

The previous dome began at the floor and described the complete building. That is the silhouette of a shelter. A silo’s roof sits on top of a visible storage cylinder.

The body disappeared.

Lowering the roof line alone produced an igloo or yurt. The next mark restores a clear roof joint and enough straight wall to establish a vertical vessel.

Three silo structures

Each option uses medium proportions: clearly taller than the prior study, but wider and calmer than the first mark.

Crown

Tower

A narrower storage cylinder with two hoops. Closest to a traditional tower silo without returning to the original height.

Architectural / compact / familiar

Recommended direction

Steel makes the cylindrical body dominant and keeps the roof shallow. It reads as a silo without borrowing the pitched-roof silhouette of a barn.

Preferred structure

Steel

The cap occupies roughly one quarter of the height. Straight walls define the storage cylinder. Two restrained hoops and a small doorway provide enough architectural scale without recreating the original grid.

  • Roof: shallow domed cap with a clear joint
  • Body: straight walls, 72% of total height
  • Detail: two hoops and one doorway
  • Proportion: broad cylinder, neither tower nor hut

Status-item proof

The shallow cap, roof joint, cylinder, and doorway survive at 16–20 px. State remains additive and never replaces the structure.
